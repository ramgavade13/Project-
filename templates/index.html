About Little Lemon

Little Lemon was founded in 2021 by two Italian brothers, Mario and Adrian. Having grown up in the sunny coastal hills of the Mediterranean, they moved to Chicago with a singular dream: to bring centuries-old family recipes and fresh, sun-kissed ingredients to American dining tables. Every dish is seasoned with love, extra virgin olive oil, and organic lemons.
